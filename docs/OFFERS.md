# Offer types

Every opportunity the Scout keeps is sorted into one of five types. The Builder drafts a listing in that shape.

| Type | What the buyer gets | Typical channel | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kit` | A ready-to-use automation, template or workflow they run themselves | Gumroad | n8n workflow that sends invoice reminders |
| `service` | A fixed-price job done by AI and checked by a human before delivery | Fiverr, direct | Turn an Excel stock sheet into a dashboard |
| `agent` | A configured agent or Claude skill they keep using | Gumroad, direct | Review-summary agent for a shop |
| `data_product` | A dataset, report or dashboard | Gumroad, direct | Monthly sector report built from public data |
| `learning` | A short guide or course teaching small firms to use AI for one job | Gumroad | "Automate your invoice follow-ups in one afternoon" |

## Rules every offer follows

- It promises only what AI agents plus one human reviewer can deliver to a high standard.
- It has a fixed scope and a fixed price.
- It makes no income claims, guarantees or fake social proof.
- A human reads it, and the source post behind it, before it is listed.

## Judging results

Drafts are not revenue. For each approved offer, track in your own sheet:

| Offer | Listed on | Date | Views | Messages | Sales | Revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

An offer with no messages after 14 days listed gets reworked or retired. A type with no sales after 30 days gets fewer slots in the feeds.
